What is art psychology?

Where creativity meets clinical care

Art psychology uses the creative process — painting, drawing, collage, clay and mixed media — alongside evidence-informed psychological practice. It helps you explore emotions, ease anxiety, process experiences and build self-awareness, even when words feel out of reach.

  • You don't need any art skills or experience
  • The process matters more than the finished piece
  • Tailored to your goals, pace and comfort
  • A private, non-judgemental environment
